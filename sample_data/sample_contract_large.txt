MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (EXCERPT) — AI-ENABLED SERVICES
Effective Date: 01 Jan 2026

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 “Services” means the AI-enabled analytics and automation services, including optional machine-learning components.
1.2 “Customer Data” means all data provided by Customer, including Personal Data.
1.3 “AI System” means any model, algorithm, or automated decision component used to generate outputs.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
2.1 Provider will deliver Services described in Statement(s) of Work.
2.2 Provider may use subcontractors for parts of the Services.

3. DATA PROCESSING & PRIVACY
3.1 Provider shall process Customer Data solely to provide the Services.
3.2 Provider will comply with all applicable privacy laws.
3.3 Provider may transfer Customer Data to other jurisdictions where it maintains facilities.
3.4 Provider will assist Customer with data subject requests upon request within a reasonable time.
3.5 Data retention: Provider will retain Customer Data for 5 years after termination for “business purposes” unless prohibited.

4. SECURITY
4.1 Provider will implement commercially reasonable security measures.
4.2 Provider may update security measures at its discretion.
4.3 Provider will encrypt data in transit; encryption at rest will be used where feasible.
4.4 Access to Customer Data will be granted to Provider personnel on a need-to-know basis.
4.5 Provider is not required to meet any specific standard (e.g., ISO 27001) unless agreed in a SOW.

5. INCIDENT NOTIFICATION
5.1 Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed security incident “promptly” after discovery.
5.2 Provider will provide reasonable cooperation; details may be limited due to legal restrictions.
5.3 Provider will not notify regulators unless required by law.

6. AUDIT & INSPECTION
6.1 Customer may request evidence of Provider’s security controls once per year.
6.2 Provider may refuse audit requests that it deems unreasonable or disruptive.
6.3 On-site audits are not permitted; only written responses will be provided.

7. LOGGING & TRACEABILITY
7.1 Provider will maintain system logs appropriate to its operations.
7.2 Provider may delete logs at any time unless required for investigations.
7.3 Provider does not warrant retention of logs beyond 30 days.

8. HUMAN OVERSIGHT & ESCALATION
8.1 Provider may use automated decision-making to optimize outcomes.
8.2 Customer may request manual review for material decisions; Provider will consider the request.
8.3 Provider retains discretion to determine whether a manual review is necessary.

9. TRANSPARENCY
9.1 Provider will provide general documentation describing the Services.
9.2 Provider is not obligated to disclose model architectures, training data, or detailed explanations for outputs.
9.3 Provider may label outputs as “AI-generated” where appropriate.

10. MODEL GOVERNANCE, QUALITY, AND MONITORING
10.1 Provider will use reasonable efforts to test models before deployment.
10.2 Provider may change models at any time to improve performance.
10.3 Provider does not commit to any specific accuracy or robustness thresholds.
10.4 Provider will monitor performance periodically.

11. SUBPROCESSORS
11.1 Provider may engage subprocessors to process Customer Data.
11.2 Provider will maintain a list of subprocessors upon request.
11.3 Provider may add new subprocessors without prior notice.

12. TERM AND TERMINATION
12.1 Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days’ notice.
12.2 Upon termination, Provider will delete Customer Data within 180 days, except where retention is required under Section 3.5.

13. LIABILITY
13.1 Provider’s total liability is capped at fees paid in the prior 3 months.
13.2 Provider is not liable for indirect or consequential damages.

14. COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY
14.1 Each party will comply with laws applicable to it.
14.2 Customer is responsible for determining whether the Services are suitable for any high-risk use case.
14.3 Provider will support reasonable compliance requests if mutually agreed.

15. GOVERNING LAW
15.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
