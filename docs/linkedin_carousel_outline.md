# LinkedIn Carousel Outline (5 slides)

## Slide 1 — Hook
“Why most GenAI contract demos fail in regulated environments”

## Slide 2 — The Problem
- Unstructured inputs
- No traceability
- No citations
- No stable integration boundary

## Slide 3 — The Solution (Agentic)
Extractor → Mapper → Auditor (Local-PDF RAG) → Verifier → Reviewer

## Slide 4 — What the output looks like
- Score + risk level
- Findings with clause evidence
- Regulation citations (doc/page/excerpt)
- Schema-validated JSON export

## Slide 5 — Takeaway
“Agentic AI + strict schemas + local grounding = production patterns, not prompt demos”
